[Overview] Show the process of finding π from the share of random points that land inside a circle, using real calculation.

[Timeline]
0–3s: Title "Scatter Points to Find π"
3–9s: A square with side 2 and a circle with radius 1. Add 10 points, 1 per beat, then accelerate
9–15s: At the top right, "π ≈ 4 × inside ÷ total" with the point counts and the estimate
15–21s: At the bottom right, a line chart of the estimate (logarithmic x-axis) converges on a dashed line at 3.14159; 10,000 points at 21s
21–25s: Show the final estimate and its difference from the true value

[Design] Background #101418, lines #E0E0E0, inside points #2EC4B6, outside #FF6B6B. Numbers in JetBrains Mono 700, other text in Inter.

[Calculation] Fixed random seed; draw cumulatively on a Canvas. Displayed values are the results of the real calculation.

[Sound] 100 BPM (1 beat = 18 frames). A click for each point. Impact hits at 3, 9, 15, and 21s.

[Output] 1920×1080, 30fps, 25s (750 frames), MP4 (H.264 + AAC), under 50MB. Visuals in Remotion; audio synthesized to WAV in Node.js. Google Fonts, no external assets.
